# Finance Review: Paid Ads (2026-10-09)

**Recommendation: Reject paid ads for now. Revisit only for buying-intent guide pages after they show organic conversions.**

| Item | Number | Source / basis |
|---|---|---|
| Cost per click, Arts & Entertainment | ~$1.63 | Industry benchmark, 2026 |
| Display ad earnings, entertainment sites | ~$2 per 1,000 pageviews | Industry benchmark |
| Ad earnings per visitor (2 pages/visit) | ~$0.004 | Calculated |
| Affiliate earnings per visitor | ~$0.0015 | **Guess:** 1% click to Amazon × 5% buy × $3 commission ($100 item at ~3%) |
| **Total earnings per visitor** | **~$0.006** | |
| **Cost per paid visitor** | **~$0.50–$1.63** | Narrow party phrases may run cheaper than the category average. Not verified |

**Verdict:** each paid visitor loses roughly $0.50–$1.60.

**Amazon 3-sale goal with paid traffic:** at the guessed conversion rates, 3 sales take about 6,000 visitors. At these click costs, that's well beyond the $50 test cap.

**What would change this:** a guide page (e.g., "best karaoke machine for parties") showing much higher affiliate conversion from organic visitors. Then a small capped test on that page alone could make sense.
