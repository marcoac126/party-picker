        How to pick a book club book with the wheel

        Before the meeting ends, ask each member to suggest one or two books. Under “What’s on the wheel”, choose “Our list”, search for each book, and spin. That’s next month’s read. Everyone had a say, and nobody has to argue for their pick.

        Ways to use it

        • One suggestion each: keeps the wheel fair and short enough to read every title.
        • Theme months: open “Filters” above the wheel and pick an occasion, a mood, or a genre, like Christmas & holidays and Cozy.
        • Stay current: “Popular this week” under Occasion is this week’s bestseller chart, refreshed every Monday.
        • Pick the host too: choose “Who hosts”, add members’ names, and turn on “Remove each pick” so everyone takes a turn.

        What makes a good book club book?

        The best picks give people something to disagree about: a character who makes a hard choice, an ending that splits the room, or a true story that raises questions. Keep length in mind, since a 300-page novel is finished by more members than a 700-page epic. Check that it’s easy to get at the library or as an ebook, too.

        Where do the suggested picks come from?

        Most are long-time book club favorites. “Popular this week” comes from Apple Books’ US bestseller chart each week, leaving out kids’ books, comics, and box sets.

        Is the wheel random?

        Yes. Each spin uses your browser’s built-in random number generator, so every book on the wheel has the same chance. Your list stays on your device. When you search for a book, only what you type is sent to Open Library, a free public book catalog.
