How to pick a book club book with the wheel

Before the meeting ends, ask each member to suggest one or two books. Type them into “Use your club’s list”, spin, and that’s next month’s read. Everyone had a say, and nobody has to argue for their pick.

Ways to use it

  • One suggestion each: keeps the wheel fair and the list short enough to read every title.
  • Theme months: deal popular picks from one genre when the club wants a change of pace.
  • Pick the host too: switch to “Who hosts”, add members’ names, and turn on “Remove each pick” so everyone takes a turn before anyone repeats.
  • Veto rule: let each member veto one spin per year. Use it wisely.

What makes a good book club book?

The best picks give people something to disagree about: a character who makes a hard choice, an ending that splits the room, or a true story that raises questions. Keep length in mind, since a 300-page novel is finished by more members than a 700-page epic. Check that it’s easy to get at the library or as an ebook, too.

Is the wheel random?

Yes. Each spin uses your browser’s built-in random number generator, so every book on the wheel has the same chance. Your list stays on your device and isn’t sent anywhere.
