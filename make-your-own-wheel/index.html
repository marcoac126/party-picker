How it works

Type each choice on its own line. The wheel updates as you type. Tap Spin, and the wheel picks one at random.

Ways to use it

  • Where to eat: list a few places and let the wheel settle it.
  • Who goes first: put everyone’s name on the wheel.
  • Taking turns: turn on “Remove each choice” so no one gets picked twice.

Is the wheel random?

Yes. Each spin uses your browser’s built-in random number generator, so every choice has the same chance. Your list stays on your device and isn’t sent anywhere.
