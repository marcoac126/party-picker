    How to run a karaoke night with the wheel

    Pass the phone around. Whoever holds it spins, and they sing what it lands on. No skipping, unless the group votes to let them off.

    Ways to play

    • Decade night: pick one decade list and stick with it all evening.
    • No repeats: turn on “Remove each song after it’s picked” so every spin is new.
    • Duet roulette: spin the duets list, then spin a second wheel of names to pick partners.
    • Crowd’s choice: have everyone add one song to the list before the first spin.

    Picking a good karaoke song

    Songs with a big, well-known chorus get the room singing along, which takes pressure off the singer. Mid-tempo songs are easier than fast ones, and a song in a comfortable range beats a show-off song every time.

    Is the wheel random?

    Yes. Each spin uses your browser’s built-in random number generator, so every song has the same chance. Your list stays on your device and isn’t sent anywhere.
