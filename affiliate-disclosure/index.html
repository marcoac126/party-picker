How the site makes money

The Party Wheel is free. Some book links are affiliate links: if you buy a book through a “Buy at Bookshop.org” link, we may earn a commission from Bookshop.org, whose fees support independent bookstores. You pay the same price either way. Pages with these links say so near the links.

Programs we’re part of

  • Bookshop.org (since October 2026): the “Buy at Bookshop.org” links on the book club picker.

We’ve also been accepted to Awin, the network that runs Libro.fm’s audiobook program, and will list Libro.fm here if its links go live. Programs we may join later include Amazon Associates; if we do, as an Amazon Associate we would earn from qualifying purchases.

Everything else is a plain link

Links to Goodreads, Libro.fm, Amazon, Audible, your library, JustWatch, and YouTube, and the links for games, movies, and gift ideas, don’t earn us anything today. They’re there to help you find what you land on.

What never changes

  • The wheel is random. No one pays to be on the wheel or to be picked. Every spin uses your browser’s random number generator.
  • Suggestions aren’t ads. Song, book, and game suggestions are chosen because they’re good fits for a party or book club, or because they’re on public bestseller charts.
  • We include free options. Where it makes sense, we link to ways to borrow or play for free, like your library.
