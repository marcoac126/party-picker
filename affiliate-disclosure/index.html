Affiliate disclosure

Last updated October 10, 2026

Right now

None of our links earn us money yet. Links to stores like Bookshop.org, Libro.fm, Amazon, and Audible are plain links that help you find a book, game, movie, or gift idea.

In the future

To keep the site free, we may join affiliate programs. That means if you buy something after following one of our links, the store may pay us a small commission. You pay the same price either way.

When that happens, we’ll list each program here, and pages with affiliate links will say so near the links. Programs we may join include Bookshop.org, Libro.fm, and Amazon Associates. As an Amazon Associate, we would earn from qualifying purchases.

What never changes

  • The wheel is random. No one pays to be on the wheel or to be picked. Every spin uses your browser’s random number generator.
  • Suggestions aren’t ads. Song, book, and game suggestions are chosen because they’re good fits for a party or book club, or because they’re on public bestseller charts.
  • We include free options. Where it makes sense, we link to ways to borrow or play for free, like your library.
