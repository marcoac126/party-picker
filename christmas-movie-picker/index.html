      How to pick a Christmas movie

      Start with who’s watching. With kids in the room, keep only “Family” and “Animated” on, since those leave out PG-13 and R-rated picks. For a grown-up night, add comedies, rom-coms, or the action picks. Spin, and the wheel shows a one-line summary plus where to stream, rent, or watch the trailer.

      Ways to use it

      • Movie night with friends: switch to “Our own list”, have everyone add one movie, and spin.
      • A movie a night in December: turn on “Remove each movie” so you never repeat one before Christmas.
      • Settling the Die Hard debate: leave “Action & offbeat” on and let the wheel decide.

      Where can I watch it?

      Streaming services shuffle their holiday movies every year, so each pick links to a JustWatch search that shows where it’s streaming right now, plus Amazon to rent or buy it.

      Is the wheel random?

      Yes. Each spin uses your browser’s built-in random number generator, so every movie on the wheel has the same chance. Your lists stay on your device and aren’t sent anywhere.
