      How to pick a white elephant gift

      Stick to the group’s price limit, then aim for something people will fight over. The most-stolen gifts are usually cozy (a soft blanket), useful (a phone charger or tumbler), or so ridiculous everyone wants it (a giant gummy bear). Spin, and the wheel shows why the idea works plus a link to shop it.

      What makes a good white elephant gift?

      • Anyone could use it. You don’t know who’ll end up with it, so skip anything too personal.
      • It gets a reaction. Funny gifts make the game more fun, as long as they’re not mean or too adult for the crowd.
      • It’s worth the limit. A gift that looks like more than it cost tends to get stolen.

      Popular price limits

      Most office and family exchanges set a limit of $20 or $25. Use “$10 to $25” for those. For a $20 limit, pick “$10 to $25” and check the price before you buy. “Under $10” works for big groups and dollar-store rules, and “$25 to $50” for friend groups that go big.

      Is the wheel random?

      Yes. Each spin uses your browser’s built-in random number generator, so every idea on the wheel has the same chance. Nothing you pick is sent anywhere.
