About The Party Wheel

The Party Wheel is a new site I launched in October 2026.

I built it because my girlfriend and I are in a few book clubs between us (I’m only in one), and sometimes it’s a challenge to pick a book. Using existing spin-the-wheel sites was a struggle too. We had to type in every book, then still track down where to find each one.

It started as a way to make book club easier. The book club wheel picks our next book, or who hosts, and links straight to where we can buy it, borrow it from the library, or listen to it. From there it grew into free wheels for karaoke (we do karaoke a few times a year), game nights, and the holidays, each with ready-made lists and links to find what you land on. You can see them all on the home page.

It’s made for book clubs, game nights, and people hosting parties, mostly on their phones. Your lists stay on your device. Nothing you type is sent to me.

Have an idea for a new wheel, or a song, book, or game I’m missing? Email hello@thepartywheel.com.
